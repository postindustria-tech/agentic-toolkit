# Additional service promises

This source adds current authoritative requirements to harbor-sow.md, without
changing its delivery windows or D1 exclusion. Both documents are current scope.

- S6 — Overdue alerts: members receive email and SMS overdue alerts.
- S7 — Equipment reservations: members can submit and cancel a reservation.
- S8 — Loan CSV export: staff export the loan register as CSV.
- S9 — Staff edit audit log: staff inspect the actor and time of each loan edit.
- S10 — Screen reader navigation: members navigate the service using a screen reader.
- S11 — Late fee calculation: staff calculate a late fee from days overdue.
- S12 — Hold notification: members receive notification when a hold becomes available.

The Jira plan uses release versions, not epics, as delivery windows. Opening day
handles registration, profile, browsing and reservation submission. Lending desk
handles loan history, alerts, reporting, navigation, fees, holds and cancellation.
Document-plan placement remains as written in harbor-sow.md; Jira may differ.

# Harbor equipment library — service agreement

## Reading this agreement

Service promises below are the authoritative scope. Delivery windows are the
plan under review. Deferred services explicitly override the corresponding
promise for this release. Acceptance of each window uses the applicable service
promises. No prior agreement is supplied.

## Service promises

- S1 — Member registration: a resident can create a library membership.
- S2 — Loan history: a member can see all previous equipment loans and return dates.
- S3 — Email change: a member can change the email address on their profile.
- S4 — Deposit refund: staff can return a deposit to a member's payment card.
- S5 — Equipment catalogue: a resident can browse available equipment.

## Delivery windows

### Opening day

- Member registration.
- Member profile maintenance.
- Equipment catalogue.

Opening day focuses on member self-service and browsing; it does not include
loan records or payments.

### Lending desk

- Record equipment checkout and return.
- Weather forecast widget.

The desk window is for recording individual loan transactions. It does not
promise a member-facing page for viewing past loans or returning deposits.

## Deferred services

- D1 — Deposit refund (S4) is excluded from this release; staff refunds remain manual.
